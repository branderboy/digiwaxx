Frequently Asked Questions

  
Should I send this pitch to hundreds of DJs?
No — personal pitches are for the handful of DJs you have real context with. For scale, service the record through the pool: one submission reaches 30,000+ DJs in the channel they already check.

  
Why does the pitch mention versions and BPM?
Because those are the first two practical questions a working DJ has. Answering them up front signals you know how DJs work — which is most of the battle for a reply.
