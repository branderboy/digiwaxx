Frequently Asked Questions

How long should an artist bio be?

Three lengths cover every use: a one-liner for social profiles, 2–3 sentences for streaming platforms, and 100–150 words for press and EPKs. Longer than that, nobody reads.

What makes a bio actually work?

Specifics: your city, your sound named precisely, and one verifiable proof point. "Versatile artist making waves" says nothing; "Houston melodic rap with club spins across Texas" books shows.
