With AddAGuestPost, your moat is **authority**.

With Digiwaxx, your moat is **access**.

You have direct access to:

* 30,000+ DJs
* Radio programmers
* Playlist opportunities
* Industry personalities
* Decades of credibility since 1998

Most competitors have one of those. Digiwaxx has several.

So I'd position every page around one core promise:

> **Get your music into the hands of people who still influence what gets played, shared, and discovered.**

Everything else—record pool placement, DJ blasts, playlist outreach, radio rotation—is evidence that you can deliver on that promise.

If I were building this campaign, I'd aim for **300–500 high-intent pages**, not 10,000. Every page would target artists who are already looking for promotion, education, or launch help, and every one would naturally funnel into the submission form. That's the same strategy that makes AddAGuestPost scalable without turning it into a generic content site.
