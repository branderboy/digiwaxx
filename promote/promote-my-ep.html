Promote My EP

How do I promote my ep?

Quick Answer

Promote a ep by putting it in front of people who play music for audiences: treat it as 2 to 3 single campaigns sharing one identity, service the lead track to DJs, follow with the second focus track, and let the EP collect the audience. One Digiwaxx campaign services it to 30,000+ DJs with radio, playlist, and coverage layers on top.

The EP is the independent artist’s best format: enough songs to show range, few enough that every track can get real attention. The right promotion plan treats an EP as a short season of singles with a shared cover story.

What a EP Campaign Includes

  • Record pool placement, your record serviced to the Digiwaxx network of 30,000+ club, mixshow, and radio DJs
  • Spotify playlist placement and Digiwaxx radio rotation
  • DJ blast email feature, direct to the DJ list that has trusted Digiwaxx since 1998
  • Official Digiwaxx.com artist coverage, an SEO-indexed artist spotlight that keeps working after the campaign
  • Pro and Elite add socials pushes, featured DJ Call spins, a one-on-one interview, and a performance snapshot report

How It Works for a EP

Service the lead track to the DJ network 2 weeks before the EP drops; release the EP; then campaign the second focus track 4 to 6 weeks later as a fresh news moment on the same project. Two waves, one body of work, and every listener either wave earns lands on a page with more to play.

Why DJs First

Every format benefits from the same physics: DJs are the last audiences with no skip button. A ep with real DJ activity behind it generates the searches, saves, and clips that make every other channel, playlists, radio, press, bookings, easier to win. That access is what you are buying: one submission, the whole network.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many tracks should an EP have?

Four to six is the sweet spot, enough to feel like a project, small enough that your two focus tracks are half the tracklist.

EP or three singles?

If the songs share a sound and story, the EP gives you a bigger artifact for press and coverage while still allowing single-style campaigns per track. If they don’t belong together, release them as singles.
