Promote My Album

How do I promote my album?

Quick Answer

Promote a album by putting it in front of people who play music for audiences: lead with the strongest 1 to 2 focus tracks serviced to DJs, run the album as a story across 8 to 12 weeks, and let each single pull listeners into the full project. One Digiwaxx campaign services it to 30,000+ DJs with radio, playlist, and coverage layers on top.

Albums fail on streaming when they are promoted as one event. They win when they are promoted as a season: focus tracks carry the campaign, the album is the destination, and every DJ spin or playlist add on a single sells the whole body of work.

What a Album Campaign Includes

  • Record pool placement, your record serviced to the Digiwaxx network of 30,000+ club, mixshow, and radio DJs
  • Spotify playlist placement and Digiwaxx radio rotation
  • DJ blast email feature, direct to the DJ list that has trusted Digiwaxx since 1998
  • Official Digiwaxx.com artist coverage, an SEO-indexed artist spotlight that keeps working after the campaign
  • Pro and Elite add socials pushes, featured DJ Call spins, a one-on-one interview, and a performance snapshot report

How It Works for a Album

Pick the one or two records DJs can actually play, club energy, radio-clean, and campaign those through the network while the album provides the depth behind them. Coverage and interview layers (Pro/Elite) do more for albums than any other format: a project gives you a story worth telling, and an SEO-indexed feature gives that story a permanent home.

Why DJs First

Every format benefits from the same physics: DJs are the last audiences with no skip button. A album with real DJ activity behind it generates the searches, saves, and clips that make every other channel, playlists, radio, press, bookings, easier to win. That access is what you are buying: one submission, the whole network.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I promote every song on the album?

No, campaign the 1 to 2 focus tracks hard and let the album absorb the attention. You can run a second focus track 6 to 8 weeks later as a fresh wave.

Before or after the album drops?

Start the first focus-track campaign 2 to 3 weeks before the album, then work post-release waves. Albums reward longer campaigns than singles.
