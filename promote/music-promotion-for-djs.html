Music Promotion for DJs

How do djs use Digiwaxx?

Quick Answer

For DJs, Digiwaxx is the supply line: a curated feed of new records in DJ-ready form, clean, dirty, instrumental, correctly tagged, from labels and serious independents, plus the platform where your feedback helps decide what breaks next.

Digiwaxx has been a DJ-side service since 1998, the pool exists because working DJs need a steady, trusted source of playable records, and the artist side exists because that DJ network is real. If you play out, clubs, radio, mixshows, mobile, this is the room where new records reach you first.

What DJs Get

  • DJ-ready files: clean/dirty/instrumental versions, correct metadata and BPM, records you can drop into tonight's set
  • First access: records serviced to the pool often land before public release
  • A voice in what breaks: your downloads and feedback are the reaction data that moves records up the chain
  • The network effect: 30,000+ working peers, the crates you check are the crates everyone checks
