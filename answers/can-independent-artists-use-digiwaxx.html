Can Independent Artists Use Digiwaxx?

Can independent artists use Digiwaxx, or is it labels only?

Quick Answer

Yes, independent artists are the majority of Digiwaxx campaigns. You need a finished, mastered record (ideally clean, dirty, and instrumental versions), $99 to $199 one-time, and no label, contract, or connections. The network judges records, not rosters.

Record pools began as label infrastructure, which is why the question is fair, but the modern pool democratized exactly that access. Digiwaxx has serviced independent records alongside label releases for years: same network, same packaging standards, same feedback loop. What is required is professionalism, not a deal.

What You Need (and Don’t)

  • Need: a mastered record; clean/dirty/instrumental versions; final artwork; correct metadata
  • Don’t need: a label, a manager, prior releases, follower counts, or anyone’s permission

The packaging bar exists because DJs are professionals, meet it and your record sits in the same crates as major releases.

What a Campaign Looks Like for an Independent

Pick a tier (Starter $99, Pro $149, Elite $199, one-time, no contracts), submit your files, and the record is serviced to the 30,000+ DJ network with radio rotation, playlist placement, and an SEO-indexed artist feature layered in. You keep 100% of your masters, royalties, and rights, the campaign never touches ownership. Then read what happens after you submit and work the reactions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a certain number of streams or followers to qualify?

No. DJs evaluate the record, not your metrics, which is precisely why the DJ channel is the fairest arena an unknown artist can compete in.

Does Digiwaxx take any rights or royalties?

No, campaigns are a one-time promotion fee. Masters, publishing, and royalties stay entirely yours.
