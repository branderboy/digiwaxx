What Happens After You Submit Music to a Record Pool

What actually happens after I submit my music?

Quick Answer

After submission your record is reviewed and packaged, serviced to the DJ network, and surfaced to DJs by format. DJs download and test it; downloads, feedback, and spins flow back — becoming the reaction data you use for every next move.

Submission is the start of a pipeline, not a lottery ticket. Understanding the stages — and what you should be doing during each — turns a passive wait into an active campaign. Here is the lifecycle of a serviced record, from upload to the feedback loop.

Stage 1: Review & Packaging (Days 1–3)

The record is checked for quality and completeness: audio standards, versions (clean/dirty/instrumental), artwork, metadata. This is where submissions with only an explicit version or missing tags lose most of their potential reach — fix that before submitting and everything downstream widens.

Stage 2: Servicing (Week 1)

The record goes out to the network, filed by genre and format so the right DJs find it — a club DJ in Houston, a mixshow jock in Chicago, and a mobile DJ in DC each browsing their own lanes. On the Digiwaxx network that is 30,000+ DJs with access to the record at once.

Stage 3: Pickup & Testing (Weeks 1–4)

DJs download what fits their rooms and test it — a Friday night club slot, a Saturday mixshow, a college party. Your job in this window: be visible. Post about the record, thank DJs who engage, and have your socials ready for the "who is this?" searches that real-world plays create.

Stage 4: The Feedback Loop

Downloads, ratings, comments, and spin activity come back. Read it like a professional: which cities, which formats, which version. That data drives wave two — curator pitches with receipts, mixshow pushes in reacting markets, content built on real clips, and bookings where the record already works.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long until DJs start playing my record?

Downloads typically begin within days of servicing; club tests and mixshow spins follow over 2–4 weeks. Records keep getting discovered inside the network well beyond that window.

What should I do while my record is being serviced?

Work the visibility layer: content around the record, engaged socials, updated profiles — and respond fast to any DJ interaction. Service creates the exposure; you convert it.

Does every submitted record get played?

No honest service guarantees spins — DJs choose what works for their rooms. What service guarantees is qualified exposure: your record in front of thousands of working DJs who are actively looking for new music.
