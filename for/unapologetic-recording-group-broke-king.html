Our U.S. DJ, Club and Radio Network

The Digiwaxx record pool has been serving records to DJs since 1998. Your release goes to a network of 30,000+ U.S. DJs working across four settings:

  • Club DJs: the rooms where a record is tested on a crowd first
  • Mixshow DJs: the DJs who bridge the club and the air
  • Radio DJs: clean versions serviced for broadcast
  • Mobile DJs: events, parties and private bookings

Genres we work: hip hop, R&B, afrobeats, amapiano and afro house, dancehall, reggae, Latin and gospel.

U.S. markets we work city by city: New York, Atlanta, Houston, Los Angeles, Chicago, Miami, Washington DC and the DMV, Philadelphia, Dallas, Detroit, Memphis, New Orleans, the Bay Area, Baltimore, Charlotte and Nashville.
