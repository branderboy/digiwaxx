How to Get Booked for Shows

How do I get booked to perform?

Quick Answer

Promoters book risk-reduction: proof you draw or proof your record moves rooms. Build a bookable package, a working club record, live footage, real local numbers, and start where you have leverage: your own city, DJs who already spin you, and openers.

A booking is a bet by a promoter that you will add value to their night, bodies at the door or energy in the room. New artists rarely have a draw, so the fastest substitute is a record that already works in the room: when club DJs are spinning your song, the promoter’s risk drops to zero and the "can he perform it live?" conversation starts naturally.

The Club Record → Booking Pipeline

This is the most reliable independent booking route: get the record serviced to DJs → the record reacts in local rooms → DJs and promoters (who are often the same people or one phone call apart) put you on the flyer to perform the song the room already knows. Your record works as your advance team every weekend you are not there.

Build the Bookable Package

  • Live footage, even one well-shot 60-second clip of you commanding any stage
  • A one-pager (EPK): bio, the record and its activity (spins, streams, clips), photos, contact
  • Real numbers, honestly presented, local monthly listeners and a reactive record beat inflated totals
  • Professionalism signals: on time, sound-check ready, set exactly as long as asked

Where the First Bookings Come From

  • Openers for touring acts in your city (promoters fill these locally)
  • DJ-run nights where your record already spins
  • College shows and showcases with real crowds
  • Your own show: co-headline with 2 to 3 local artists and pool audiences, promoters watch who can fill a room on their own steam

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I charge for a show?

Early on, price for the relationship: free-to-modest for strategic slots, and let fees follow your draw. When your record is moving in clubs, your leverage, and your fee, rises with it.

Do I need a booking agent?

Agents come after demand exists, they respond to inbound interest, they rarely create it. Until then, you (or a hustling friend as de facto manager) are the booking department.
