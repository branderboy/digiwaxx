How to Get Playlist Placement

How do I get my music placed on playlists?

Quick Answer

Legitimate placement comes three ways: pitch editorial free through Spotify for Artists pre-release, earn independent curator adds with fit and momentum, and trigger algorithmic playlists through engagement. Anyone selling guaranteed placement is selling policy violations or bots.

The Scam Filter (Read First)

Rules of thumb that save careers: nobody legitimate sells a guaranteed add on a specific Spotify playlist; any playlist whose followers are huge but whose adds produce no streams is botted; and Spotify actively removes artificial streams and can penalize your track. If a "curator" quotes a price per placement, walk.

What Curators Actually Respond To

  • Fit: you clearly listened to the playlist before pitching
  • Momentum: spins, clips, growth, proof other people are reacting
  • Brevity: two sentences and a link beat a press release
  • Reciprocity: you follow, share, and credit the playlists that support you

The Placement Flywheel

Placement begets placement: DJ activity and content create streams → streams and saves trigger algorithmic playlists → algorithmic performance and visible momentum make curator pitches land → curator adds feed the algorithm further. Note where that flywheel starts: real-world activity around the record. Start the motion offline, harvest it online.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find independent playlist curators?

Search your genre and mood terms on Spotify, check credits on similar artists’ "Discovered On" sections, and use curator platforms with transparent review models. Prioritize playlists whose follower counts and stream results look organic.

Does playlist placement still matter in the algorithm era?

Yes, but as one channel, not the goal. Algorithmic surfaces now drive more discovery than most editorial lists, and they are fed by engagement, which real-world exposure creates.
