How to Get Radio Spins

How do I get radio spins on my record?

Quick Answer

Spins are earned bottom-up: college and community stations first, then commercial mixshows, both programmed by DJs you can actually reach. Bring a broadcast-clean version, trackable metadata, and evidence the record reacts. Rotation follows reported mixshow activity.

A "spin" is a logged broadcast play, and monitored spins are currency, they chart, they impress programmers, and they prove a market. The mistake is aiming at drive-time rotation directly. Rotation is the last domino: it falls after mixshow DJs, who are club DJs with a radio shift, have already made the record familiar in the market.

The Spin Ladder

  1. College/community radio: submit properly, follow each station's process, do the interviews, these spins are real and reportable
  2. Mixshows: reached through DJ channels, not station switchboards; a record already serviced to and reacting with club DJs walks in warm
  3. Specialty/local shows: your city's own-artist hours, the easiest commercial-station door
  4. Rotation: music directors add what mixshows, streaming, and clubs have already validated

Make Every Spin Count

  • Register the record so monitoring services can detect it (correct ISRC/metadata)
  • Collect airchecks, a clip of your record on air is gold for socials and future pitches
  • Thank the DJ publicly and privately; radio people move stations and remember artists who respect the craft

Why DJ Service Comes First

Mixshow DJs discover music the same way club DJs do, through pools and peer crates. Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as an official radio spin?

A broadcast play logged by a monitoring service on a tracked station. That is why metadata and registration matter, an untracked play helps the room hear you but never shows up on paper.

Can an independent artist really get commercial radio play?

Through mixshows and specialty shows, yes, routinely. Sustained daytime rotation usually requires a story (regional buzz, streaming growth, club activity) and often a radio promo push, but every such story started with the first mixshow spins.
