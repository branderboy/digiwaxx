How Many Songs Should You Release?

How many songs should an independent artist release, and how often?

Quick Answer

For most independent artists: 6–12 well-promoted singles a year beats one album. A single every 4–8 weeks keeps algorithms fed and gives every record its own promotion window — but only release at the pace you can actually promote.

The streaming era rewards consistent presence over big drops. Each release is a fresh chance at algorithmic playlists, a new pitch to DJs and curators, and a new reason to talk to your audience. But cadence without promotion is just uploading — three singles promoted properly will do more than twelve released into silence.

The Case for Singles First

Until you have an audience that shows up on release day, albums bury your best songs. Ten tracks released at once get one promotion cycle; the same ten songs as singles get ten. Singles also give you ten rounds of DJ service, ten editorial pitches, and ten data points on what your audience responds to.

A Cadence That Works

  • Every 4–8 weeks: a single, fully worked — DJ service, content, curator pitches
  • Between drops: remixes, acoustic versions, visualizers — new surfaces for the same record
  • Once a year at most: a project (EP/album) that packages your proven singles plus new material, once there is an audience waiting for it

Quality Floor, Not Quality Ceiling

The rule is not "release everything." It is: never release below your floor (mastered, properly packaged, something you would play for a DJ), and do not sit on records for a mythical perfect moment. A good record promoted this quarter beats a great record promoted never.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it bad to release music too often?

Only if promotion can’t keep up. Back-to-back releases that each get real promotion compound; weekly uploads that get none train your audience — and the algorithm — to ignore you.

Should I drop an album as an independent artist?

After you have run enough single cycles to know 500+ people will show up on day one. Before that, the album format works against you on streaming.
