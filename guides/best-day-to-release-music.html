The Best Day to Release Music

What day of the week should I release my single?

Quick Answer

Friday is the industry standard — it aligns with New Music Friday playlists and the chart tracking week. But independent artists with no playlist shot sometimes do better midweek, when there is less major-label noise competing for attention.

Global Release Day has been Friday since 2015, which is why editorial playlists refresh and major labels drop on Fridays. That cuts two ways for an independent artist: Friday puts you in the flow where listeners expect new music, but it also puts you in the same 24 hours as every major release. The right answer depends on what your single is trying to win.

Release on Friday If…

  • You pitched Spotify editorial — New Music Friday consideration effectively requires it
  • You care about chart weeks (the tracking week runs Friday to Thursday, so a Friday release gets a full seven days)
  • Your audience already checks for new music on Fridays

Consider Midweek If…

  • You are building purely on your own audience and DJ activity, not editorial playlists
  • You want press and curator inboxes that are not flooded with Friday releases
  • Your release is a warm-up single, freestyle, or content play rather than a chart push

The Day Matters Less Than the Two Weeks Before It

An honest ranking of what moves outcomes: pre-release DJ service, a real content plan, and pitch lead time all matter more than the day of the week. Whatever day you choose, choose it three-plus weeks out so distribution, playlist pitching, and DJ service all land on time. For clubs specifically, remember DJs program for weekends — records serviced early in the week get pulled into that weekend's crates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Friday still the best day to release music?

For anything with playlist or chart ambitions, yes — Friday is the global release day and editorial playlists refresh then. For audience-only releases, midweek can buy you quieter attention.

What time should my song go live?

Midnight in your listeners’ local time on most DSPs. Plan your release-day checklist around verifying availability at midnight and pushing hard in the first 24 hours.
